About

I’m Deepak, an industrial and product designer from M. S. Ramaiah University of Applied Sciences, focused on automotive form and digital surface modelling.

At Maruti Suzuki I worked on alloy and steel wheel design. I build surfaces in Autodesk Alias, Fusion 360 and Blender, and take ideas from sketch to render to physical prototype. I started in photography. Lighting products taught me how a surface catches light, and I check every model the same way.

  1. Ideation
    Silhouette doodles and quick colour roughs, narrowed to one split-fairing direction.
    2. 
      
  2. Surface modelling
    Fairing, tank and tail built as separate panels, then joined into one continuous body.
    3. 
      
  3. Wireframe
    Topology checked from every view before rendering and printing.
    4. 
      
  4. Renders
    Gloss red paint against carbon and matte black, lit to show the fairing’s highlight lines.
    5. 
      
  5. Prototype build
    Parts 3D-printed, filled, sanded, primed and painted by hand.
    6. 
      
  6. Finished model
    The painted scale model on display.

03

      
Alloy wheel concept

      
A concept wheel developed from a Pinterest reference into a full 3D model, with Class-A surfaces built in Autodesk Alias and renders in KeyShot.

      
    

    
    
    
      
  1. Concept
    The direction came from a turbine-style wheel found on Pinterest: blades that sweep from the hub to the rim and read as motion even when the car is parked.
    2. 
      
  2. Class-A surfacing in Alias
    Each spoke built as a swept blade and joined to the hub and rim with continuous transitions, then checked with zebra stripes until the highlights ran clean.
    3. 
      
  3. Renders
    Carbon-finish spokes, a machined rim and a red brake caliper behind the blades.
    4.

04

      
Jetboard joystick controller

      
Designed at Heed Jetboards, Mysuru, in March 2025: a one-handed throttle controller for an electric jetboard.

      
    

    
    
    
      
  1. Research
    Studied existing e-foil and jetboard remotes for grip, trigger travel, battery size and waterproofing.
    2. 
      
  2. Sketches
    Concept sketches focused on how a wet hand holds and squeezes the controller.
    3. 
      
  3. 3D model
    Modelled in Fusion 360 around the battery and PCB, with the trigger placed for thumb and finger reach.
    4. 
      
  4. Beyond the controller
    At Heed I also designed and animated the jet-drive propeller mechanism, and contributed to a life jacket for riders.
